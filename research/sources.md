# Source register

Single register of every source cited by `src/data/*`. IDs are stable: **S01–S27** keep the IDs of the research bible v2.0 §I; **S28+** de-duplicate the remaining sources from §24.5 (V*), §12.10 (G*, J*) and §24.7 (PICK-*). The machine-readable copy is `src/data/evidence.ts`; a test keeps the two in sync (same IDs, same order).

Rules carried from the bible (docs/reference/tardis-research.md):

- **Source independence (§B):** two pages reproducing the same transcript are one textual witness, not two measurements.
- **Evidence priority (build plan):** footage → production drawings → official BBC → designer commentary → set photography → measured sets → reconstruction communities → secondary sites → fan speculation. Never silently promote a fan reconstruction to canon.
- **Rights:** sources are linked, never embedded. No episode frames or show screenshots are committed; CC-licensed photos keep their attribution and licence terms if ever used.
- **Scale:** no source here supplies a verified metric dimension for the 2012–17 set (§C, §18.1). S14 and S25 are other eras and must not be used for Pickwoad scale.
