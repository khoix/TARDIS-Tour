## Key decisions

- **B37 engine bypass (INF-E):** with B28 disabled the engine is unreachable by ordinary architecture, so an enclosed, fully modeled maintenance route M-02 → ENG-01 is added. It must never be presented as filmed (bible §F1).
- **B23 ARS service loop (INF-D):** prevents a dead-end ARS and gives the deep route an alternative path.
- **B06 vs B05:** the under-console hatch and the lower-wall panel stay separate anchors until footage proves a shared passage (bible §12.2 item 9).
- **B36 (INF-E, deferred):** the sub-console compartment stays a closed bay in v1.
- **Reported but unconnected doors:** C-U.upper-door-2 and C-L.lower-door-2 are modeled closed (reported=4, verified=null).
- **Storeroom placement (B17):** follows the §17 register (maintenance spine) rather than the earlier Appendix L §5.3 cultural-side proposal; both are inference.
