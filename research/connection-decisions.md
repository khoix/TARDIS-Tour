# Connection decisions

Every build-graph edge that is **not** established directly by primary evidence, with its reasoning. Machine-readable copy: `src/data/connections.ts` (`inference` field); a test requires these fields on every inferred or D/E-graded edge. Source IDs: `research/sources.md`.

Status: `proposed` = register hypothesis; `accepted` = required by a project invariant. Execution 4 built and validated a geometry for every v1 edge (see [Built paths](#built-paths-execution-4)). That shows each edge can be built, not that it is evidenced, so the statuses are unchanged.

**Never derive door adjacency from an editor's scene cut** (bible §16.2). `edited_sequence` means narrative order only.

## Built paths (Execution 4)

Profiles: _standard_ is a hex corridor 4 NU wide and 4 NU tall on a 3-NU deck; _narrow_ is 3.5 × 3.5 on a 2.5-NU deck. Every flight drops one 7-NU level over a 7-NU run, the slope of the console stairs. Only B37 drops two levels, by a 14-NU ladder shaft. Thresholds into cultural rooms are rectangular frames; all others are hex frames. Passage meshes carry the edge's provenance class (`inferred`, `sourced`, `speculative`) and the greybox tone of the destination's region.

## Edges without inference records

Directly supported by evidence (no authored architecture claimed beyond normalized geometry): B01 (shown_threshold), B02 (continuous_visible_traversal), B03 (continuous_visible_traversal), B06 (shown_threshold), B26 (edited_sequence), B28 (portal). ## Key decisions

- **B37 engine bypass (INF-E):** with B28 disabled the engine is unreachable by ordinary architecture, so an enclosed, fully modeled maintenance route M-02 → ENG-01 is added. It must never be presented as filmed (bible §F1).
- **B23 ARS service loop (INF-D):** prevents a dead-end ARS and gives the deep route an alternative path.
- **B06 vs B05:** the under-console hatch and the lower-wall panel stay separate anchors until footage proves a shared passage (bible §12.2 item 9).
- **B36 (INF-E, deferred):** the sub-console compartment stays a closed bay in v1.
- **Reported but unconnected doors:** C-U.upper-door-2 and C-L.lower-door-2 are modeled closed (reported=4, verified=null).
- **Storeroom placement (B17):** follows the §17 register (maintenance spine) rather than the earlier Appendix L §5.3 cultural-side proposal; both are inference.
